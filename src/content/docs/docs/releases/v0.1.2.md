This release wires the fresh independent batch into the evaluation workflow. The
diagnosis engine is unchanged from v0.1.1. No verification gate, promotion rule,
or abstention rule was modified.

## What changed

- `.github/workflows/final-eval.yml` now runs `fresh10-manifest.json` in addition
  to the earlier exposed holdout. It reports verified case count and exact
  function hits in the job summary, and uploads the fresh batch results.
- The ten-case fresh batch stays frozen and marked unexposed. It is a validation
  set, not a tuning target.

## Accuracy status

| Set | Cases | Exact-function matches | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Paired development cases | 6 | 6 of 6 with tracing enabled, 5 of 6 without | Development-exposed |
| Early verified regressions | 5 | 3 of 5 after tuning | Development-exposed |
| **Fresh independent batch** | **10** | **3 of 10, one abstention** | **Frozen, unexposed** |

The 3 of 10 figure is the current honest independent signal for the engine as
shipped in this release. Full table: `evaluation/holdout/FRESH10-RESULTS.md`.
History: `evaluation/final-mission/INDEPENDENT-RESULTS.md`.

This release does not claim production-ready root-cause diagnosis. Caeser remains
an investigation tool that establishes what evidence shows and ranks functions
worth inspecting.

## Get started

Build from source with Go 1.26:

```sh
git clone https://github.com/caeser-org/ceaser.git
cd caeser
go build -o caeser ./cmd/caeser
./caeser version
./caeser init
./caeser evidence --ci-log failed.log > report.json
./caeser brief --report report.json
```

For continuous integration, use the reusable workflow documented in
`docs/ci-integration.md`, pinned to `v0.1.2`.

## Next work

developed on other cases and then evaluated once on a further fresh batch.
